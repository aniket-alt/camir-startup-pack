Why the quadrant is open

The axes

  • X — hosted catalog ↔ self-hosted pool
  • Y — vendor-set, opaque ↔ customer-set, measured
  • No hosted incumbent can cross X: their product is the catalog

The honest reason

  • Customer-set tolerance caps what a vendor can claim
  • An open counterfactual caps what it can bill
  • Defensible against copying; exposed to "is this a business?"
