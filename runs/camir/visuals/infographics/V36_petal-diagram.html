Only one of five adjacent markets holds real, approved budget

Blank's petal asks which markets CAMIR draws customers from — and the primary competitor in every deal turns out to be the customer's own engineer.

The petals [petal_diagram.md]

Petal 1 — the inference bill

  • The only real, approved, transferable budget
  • Share-of-savings reaches into it

Petal 2 — gateways

  • Budget belongs to teams that chose not to self-host
  • A channel, not a wallet

Petal 3 — efficiency tooling

  • Caching, quantisation: free software
  • Shrinks CAMIR's volume

Petal 4 — FinOps

  • A pricing precedent [S38][S39]
  • And an acquirer profile

Petal 5 — own engineers

  • "I could build this in three weeks"
  • The competitor in every deal
Price out of Petal 1 or not at all. Every other pricing model asks the buyer to open a new line item — a much longer sale than shrinking one that exists.
